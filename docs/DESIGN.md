## Brand & Style

This design system drives the official showcase and digital storefront for products crafted by Warga Binaan Pemasyarakatan (WBP) of Lembaga Pemasyarakatan (Lapas) Kelas IIA Subang, functioning under the Ministry of Immigration and Corrections of the Republic of Indonesia. 

The emotional tone balances institutional prestige with restorative human dignity and civic pride:
- **Dignified & Institutional:** Radiates state integrity, quality assurance, and lawful rehabilitation through deep marine authority complemented by refined state gold accents.
- **Restorative & Inspiring:** Elevates inmate vocational creations (woodcraft, textiles, metalwork, bakery, agricultural goods) from correctional perception to certified artisanal excellence.
- **Commercial Clarity & Trust:** Conveys authentic provenance, transparent pricing, verified artisan origin, and seamless public ordering for retail buyers, corporate sponsors, and government procurement.

The visual style unites **Modern Institutional Commerce** with **Crisp Curatorial Exhibition**:
- Clear, content-first grid architectures framed by navy header structures and generous slate canvas whitespace.
- Restrained luxury accents through state gold verification seals, artisan hallmark tags, and certified correctional workshop emblems.
- High-fidelity product display cards with crisp white surface elevations, structured parameter grids, inventory status indicators, and direct procurement channels.

## Colors

The color palette establishes statutory trust while supporting high-clarity transactional commerce, rigorously conforming to WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA contrast ratios.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary Navy (`#0F254A`):** The institutional anchor. Applied to official navigation headers, checkout buttons, price totals, brand hallmarks, and core categorical navigation.
- **Secondary State Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Represents certified state accreditation and artisanal quality. Strictly applied to "Karya Terverifikasi Lapas" insignias, featured collection badges, promotional borders, and craftsmanship award markers.
- **Tertiary Slate (`#64748B`):** Governs product specifications, artisan workshop metadata, secondary actions, SKU identifiers, and deactivated controls.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F4F6F9`) & Crisp Card Containers (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces elevate vibrant product photography over the calm, glare-free institutional gray foundation.
- **Semantic Indicators:**
  - *Ready Stock / Verified (`#10B981`):* In stock, ready to dispatch, confirmed artisan provenance.
  - *Pre-Order / Limited Batch (`#F59E0B`):* Made-to-order woodcraft or batch production in progress.
  - *Out of Stock / Restocking (`#EF4444`):* Materials awaiting replenishment, workshop capacity reached.

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** consistently across all digital touchpoints. Its balanced geometric construction and tall x-height deliver immediate readability on diverse viewports, from desktop procurement dashboards to mobile catalog shoppers.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Editorial Showcases & Banners:** Large display sizes use tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) with heavy weight (`800`) to assert institutional dignity without crowding adjacent visual assets.
- **Pricing & Dimension Figures:** All currency values (e.g., `Rp 185.000`), stock counters, and material specs must utilize tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment across dense product lists.
- **Accreditation Stamps & Labels:** Micro-labels, SKU chips, and correctional workshop badges feature uppercase semi-bold treatment with positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) for enhanced legibility on high-density surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an 8-point spatial model designed to optimize catalog scanning, dimensional previews, and commerce flows.

### Breakpoints and Grid Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid, max-width of 1280px, 24px gutters, and 32px canvas margins. The product showcase uses a persistent 3-column filter sidebar paired with a 9-column responsive product card grid (3 cards per row). Product details feature a 6-column sticky visual gallery opposite a 6-column specification and purchase panel.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Product catalogs adapt to a 2-column card matrix; filters tuck into a sticky sliding drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px margins and 12px gutters. Product listings shift to 1- or 2-column cards. Sticky bottom action sheets lock order actions to the viewport edge.

### Spacing Distribution
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Attribute pairings, badge icon-to-text offsets, and pricing sub-labels.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal padding for product cards, form input groupings, and spec table rows.
- `space-lg` (24px): Separation between showcase categories, card header partitions, and order modal wrappers.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section transitions between curator collections, institutional disclaimers, and procurement guidelines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface layering, structural outline boundaries, and gentle navy-tinted ambient shadows to preserve clean institutional credibility.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Base `#F4F6F9`. Non-interactive surface grounding the entire catalog.
- **Level 1 (Crisp Product Containers & Tables):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px structural outline in `#E2E8F0` and subtle ambient diffusion: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 37, 74, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 37, 74, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover Elevation & Selected Artifacts):** Elevated shadow: `0 8px 16px -2px rgba(15, 37, 74, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 37, 74, 0.04)`. Card outlines transition smoothly to `#CBD5E1`. Used on hovered product cards, active filter pills, and drop-down menus.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Verified Preview Windows, Quick Buy Drawer):** Elevated ambient depth: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 37, 74, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 37, 74, 0.04)` finished with a 2px top accent line in State Gold (`#D4AF37`).

## Shapes

The design system implements a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** standard. This preserves an orderly, authoritative architecture while presenting consumer-facing goods warmly and accessibly.

### Geometry Usage
- **Inputs, Quantity Selectors, Buttons:** 8px (`0.5rem`). Delivers clean tactile buttons that feel deliberate and dependable.
- **Product Cards, Gallery Framing, Spec Modules:** 16px (`1rem`). Keeps dense craftsmanship imagery and item data neatly contained.
- **Modals, Verified Certificates, Bottom Sheets:** 24px (`1.5rem`). Frames provenance credentials and workshop profiles with prominent visual stature.
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Exclusively reserved for verification chips, category filter chips, stock status pills, and artisan workshop badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., Pesan Produk / Beli Sekarang):** Background `#0F254A`, text `#FFFFFF`, height 48px, border-radius 8px. Hover shifts to `#162E5B`. Includes contextual leading icons (WhatsApp, Shopping Bag, File Invoice).
- **Secondary Action (e.g., Lihat Detail / Hubungi Bimbingan Kerja):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1.5px solid border in `#0F254A`, text `#0F254A`. Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Tertiary / Filter Reset:** Ghost button, `#64748B` typography, transparent background, highlighting with `#F1F5F9` on hover.

### Product Cards (Etalase Cards)
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface, 16px border-radius, 1px perimeter border in `#E2E8F0`, subtle Level 1 shadow.
- **Visual Zone:** 1:1 or 4:3 high-definition image container with a soft `#F8FAFC` foundation and 12px rounded internal image corners. Overlaid with an absolute-positioned top-left category badge and top-right official verification chip.
- **Content Area:** 
  - Workshop subhead in uppercase 11px semi-bold Slate (`#64748B`).
  - Item Title in 16px semi-bold Deep Navy (`#0F254A`).
  - Pricing in 18px bold Deep Navy with tabular formatting.
  - Footer with stock status dot indicator and rapid "Lihat Detail" trigger.

### Verified Badges & Chips
- **Official WBP Certification Chip:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`), 28px height, background `#FEF9C3`, text `#854D0E`, 1px border in `#D4AF37`, featuring an official check-shield glyph.
- **Stock Availability Badges:**
  - *Ready Stock:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, leading 6px dot `#10B981`.
  - *Pre-Order (Karya Khusus):* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, leading 6px dot `#F59E0B`.
  - *Habis:* Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, leading 6px dot `#EF4444`.
- **Category Filter Chips:** 36px height, rounded-full. Inactive: `#FFFFFF` with 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `#475569` text. Active: `#0F254A` background with crisp `#FFFFFF` text and slight shadow.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Catalog Search Input:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, 48px height, 8px radius, text `#0F254A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Leading magnifying glass in `#64748B`. Focus state triggers a 1.5px border in `#0F254A` with a 3px outer glow `rgba(15, 37, 74, 0.12)`.
- **Quantity Selector:** Compact 36px segmented input with minus/plus buttons in `#F8FAFC` and center count in tabular `#0F254A`.

### Artisan Provenance Banner (Bimbingan Kerja Profil)
- A specialized card placed inside product detail views detailing the rehabilitation workshop:
  - Background `#F8FAFC`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 16px radius, featuring an institutional seal watermark.
  - Highlights: Nama Bengkel Kerja (e.g., "Bengkel Kayu & Mebel Lapas Subang"), program pembinaan notes, and quality inspection sign-off by Seksi Giatja (Kegiatan Kerja).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes (Filter Multi-Selection):** 18px square with 4px border radius. Unselected: 1.5px border `#94A3B8`, white fill. Selected: `#0F254A` fill with white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons (Order Variant / Shipping):** 18px circle. Unselected: 1.5px border `#94A3B8`. Selected: `#0F254A` perimeter with a centered 8px solid navy core.
